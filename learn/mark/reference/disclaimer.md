## D. Full disclaimer page — publish at /legal or /disclaimer

### Not a law firm
VeriClause is software operated by [INSERT LEGAL NAME]. It is not a law firm, does not practise law, and its operators are not acting as your lawyer by providing this service.

### No legal advice, no reliance
Content and reports are general information about the document you supplied, based only on that document and the rules you selected. They do not account for your business, other agreements, negotiations, or the laws of every jurisdiction that might apply. Do not rely on a report as a substitute for advice tailored to your situation.

### No guarantee of completeness
A contract review can only assess what is in the file. Missing schedules, side letters, prior agreements, and oral promises will not be reflected. Automated systems can miss or misinterpret language, especially in scanned, poor-quality, or unusually formatted files.

### Red / Amber / Green explained
- **Red — Stop:** a clause conflicts with your selected rules in a way that is usually material. Read it and get advice before proceeding.
- **Amber — Talk:** a clause is unclear, one-sided, or partly outside your rules. It is a prompt to discuss / negotiate, not a finding that it is unlawful.
- **Green — Fits your rules:** no conflict with your *selected rules* was found in the checked text. Green is **not** a statement that the contract is fair, enforceable, complete, or safe overall.

### Suggested rewrites
Rewrites are illustrative alternative wording. They have not been approved by a lawyer for your transaction and may not be enforceable or appropriate in your jurisdiction.
