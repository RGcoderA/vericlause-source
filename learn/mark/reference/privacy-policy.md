## 1. Who this covers

This Policy explains what information VeriClause collects when you use our contract reading service, why, who processes it for us, and your choices.

## 2. Information we collect

**a) Account information** — name, email address, and authentication information if you create an account.

**b) Documents you upload** — vendor contracts, NDAs, and similar files (PDF, DOCX, and any other formats we state as supported), the text extracted from them, and the rules / playbook choices you apply. **These documents may contain personal information and confidential business information about you and third parties — see Section 3.**

**c) Reports we generate for you** — plain-English readings, Red/Amber/Green results, quotes, and suggested rewrites derived from your documents.

**d) Usage & device information** — pages visited, features used, timestamps, browser/device type, IP address, and error logs, collected to operate, secure, and debug the service.

**e) Communications** — messages you send us (support, feedback).

We do not ask for, and you should not upload, government ID numbers, health records, payment card numbers, or documents about children, unless they are unavoidably inside a contract you choose to upload and you have the right to share them.

## 3. A special note on uploaded contracts

Contracts often name other people (signatories, employees, customers) and contain commercially sensitive terms. You are responsible for having the right to upload the document. We process it only to provide the reading service to you, on the basis described in our Terms of Service §4.
