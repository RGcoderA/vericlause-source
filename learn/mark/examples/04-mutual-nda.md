# SAMPLE — Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (Clean / Balanced Example)

> **SAMPLE FOR DEMONSTRATION AND TESTING IN VERICLAUSE ONLY.** This is a balanced example designed to score mostly GREEN against a standard playbook. It is not legal advice and should not be signed as-is — fill in the parties, dates, and purpose, and have it reviewed for your transaction.

**MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT**

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is made on [DATE] between:

**Party A:** [FULL LEGAL NAME], of [ADDRESS] ("Party A")
**Party B:** [FULL LEGAL NAME], of [ADDRESS] ("Party B")

Each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

## 1. Purpose
The Parties wish to discuss [DESCRIBE: e.g. "a possible vendor / partnership relationship"] (the "Purpose") and, for that Purpose, may disclose confidential information to each other.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other (the "Receiving Party") in connection with the Purpose, that is marked confidential or that, given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure, should reasonably be understood to be confidential. It includes business plans, pricing, customer lists, product plans, and technical information disclosed for the Purpose.

## 3. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was already known to the Receiving Party without a duty of confidentiality; (c) is received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

## 4. Confidentiality Obligations
The Receiving Party will: (a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose; (b) not disclose it to anyone except its employees, contractors, and advisors who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality duties at least as protective as these; and (c) protect it with at least reasonable care.

## 5. Compelled Disclosure
If the Receiving Party is legally required to disclose Confidential Information, it will, where legally permitted, promptly notify the Disclosing Party and co-operate, at the Disclosing Party's expense, to limit the disclosure. It will disclose only the portion legally required.

## 6. Term and Survival
6.1 This Agreement starts on the date above and the duty to protect Confidential Information lasts for **two (2) years** from the date that information was disclosed.
6.2 Sections on return of materials and remedies survive as stated in this Agreement.

## 7. Return of Materials
On the Disclosing Party's written request, or when discussions end, the Receiving Party will promptly return or, at the Disclosing Party's choice, securely destroy the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information and confirm destruction in writing on request. The Receiving Party may retain copies required by law or its standard backup systems, which remain subject to this Agreement.

## 8. No Licence and No Obligation
8.1 No licence or other right in any intellectual property is granted by disclosure under this Agreement.
8.2 Neither Party is obliged to proceed with any transaction, partnership, or further discussion.

## 9. Remedies
A breach or threatened breach may cause harm for which money alone may not be enough, so the Disclosing Party may seek an injunction or other equitable relief in addition to any other remedies, without proof of actual damage.

## 10. Liability
Except for a breach of Section 4, neither Party will be liable for indirect or consequential losses under this Agreement. Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for fraud.

## 11. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable in Ontario. The Parties will bring disputes in the courts of Ontario.

## 12. General
12.1 This is the entire agreement between the Parties about confidentiality for the Purpose, and it replaces prior discussions on that subject.
12.2 Changes must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
12.3 If any part of this Agreement is unenforceable, the rest continues in effect.
12.4 This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original.
