# SAMPLE — Vendor Services Agreement (Clean / Balanced Example)

> **SAMPLE FOR DEMONSTRATION AND TESTING IN VERICLAUSE ONLY.** Balanced example designed to score mostly GREEN: capped liability, fair payment, both sides can terminate, customer keeps its data, clear governing law. Not legal advice, not for signing as-is.

---

**VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT**

This Vendor Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made on [DATE] between:

**Customer:** [FULL LEGAL NAME], of [ADDRESS] (the "Customer")
**Vendor:** [FULL LEGAL NAME], of [ADDRESS] (the "Vendor")

## 1. Services
The Vendor will provide the services described in an Order Form or Statement of Work ("Services"), with reasonable skill and care, in line with good industry practice and the timetable in that document.

## 2. Term
This Agreement starts on [START DATE] and continues for **twelve (12) months** (the "Initial Term"), unless terminated earlier under Section 10. It does **not** automatically renew — renewal requires a written agreement signed by both Parties.

## 3. Fees and Payment
3.1 The Customer will pay the fees stated in the Order Form. Fees are in **Canadian dollars** unless the Order Form says otherwise.
3.2 The Vendor will invoice **monthly in arrears**. The Customer will pay each correct invoice within **thirty (30) days** of receipt ("Net 30").
3.3 Late payment interest is **1% per month** on overdue, undisputed amounts. The Customer may withhold amounts it disputes in good faith while the Parties resolve the dispute.
3.4 The Vendor may not increase fees during the Initial Term. Any increase for a later term must be notified at least 60 days in advance and will not exceed **3% per year**.

## 4. Customer Data and Privacy
4.1 "Customer Data" means data the Customer provides to the Vendor and personal information the Vendor processes for the Customer. As between the Parties, the Customer owns Customer Data.
4.2 The Vendor will process Customer Data only on the Customer's documented instructions, only to provide the Services, and will protect it with appropriate technical and organisational security measures.
4.3 The Vendor will **not** sell Customer Data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general AI models.
4.4 The Vendor will notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any event within **72 hours**, after becoming aware of a security incident affecting Customer Data, and will co-operate with the Customer's investigation.
4.5 Sub-processors are listed in [ANNEX / URL]. The Vendor will give at least 30 days' notice of a new sub-processor that will process Customer Data, and the Customer may object on reasonable privacy / security grounds.

## 5. Confidentiality
Each Party will protect the other's confidential information with at least reasonable care, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and share it only with representatives who need it and owe similar duties. These duties last **three (3) years** after disclosure. Standard exclusions (public, already known, independently developed, received without a duty) apply.

## 6. Intellectual Property
6.1 Each Party keeps its pre-existing intellectual property.
6.2 Deliverables created specifically for the Customer and paid for in full are assigned to the Customer on payment. The Vendor retains its tools, platform, and know-how, and grants the Customer a licence to use any Vendor material embedded in a deliverable as needed to use that deliverable.
6.3 The Vendor does **not** acquire any right to use the Customer's name, logo, or data except as needed to provide the Services.

## 7. Warranties
Each Party states it has authority to sign this Agreement. The Vendor warrants the Services will materially match their description and be performed professionally. If a Service materially fails, the Vendor will re-perform it or, if it cannot, refund the fees for the affected Service for the affected period.

## 8. Indemnities
8.1 The Vendor will indemnify the Customer against third-party claims that the Services, used as permitted, infringe intellectual property rights, and against claims arising from the Vendor's breach of Section 4 or 5.
8.2 The Customer will indemnify the Vendor against third-party claims arising from Customer Data provided in breach of this Agreement or of law.
8.3 The Party seeking indemnity will give prompt notice, reasonable co-operation, and control of the defence as is customary, and neither Party will settle a claim imposing obligations on the other without consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

## 9. Liability
9.1 **Cap:** Each Party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid or payable in the **twelve (12) months** before the event giving rise to the claim.
9.2 Neither Party is liable for indirect, consequential, or lost-profit damages, except to the extent they arise from a breach of confidentiality, a breach of Section 4, intellectual property infringement, or fraud.
9.3 Nothing limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 10. Termination
10.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on **sixty (60) days'** written notice.
10.2 Either Party may terminate immediately if the other materially breaches and does not cure within 30 days after written notice describing the breach, or becomes insolvent.
10.3 On termination, the Vendor will provide reasonable exit assistance for up to 30 days, return or securely delete Customer Data at the Customer's choice within 30 days, and refund prepaid fees for Services not provided.

## 11. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable in Ontario. Disputes will be heard in the courts of Toronto, Ontario.

## 12. General
Entire agreement on this subject. Changes in signed writing. Notices in writing to the addresses above. No assignment without consent, except to a successor in a sale of all or substantially all of a Party's business on notice. If any part is unenforceable, the rest continues. Electronic signatures and counterparts are valid.
