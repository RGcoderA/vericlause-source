**VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is made on [DATE] between:

**Customer:** [COMPANY] (the "Customer")
**Vendor:** NorthPeak Solutions Inc. (the "Vendor")

## 1. Services
The Vendor will provide such services as it reasonably determines, which it may change, substitute, or discontinue at any time in its sole discretion without notice.

## 2. Term and Renewal
This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for **thirty-six (36) months**. It will **automatically renew for successive 36-month terms** unless the Customer gives notice of non-renewal at least **twelve (12) months** before the end of the then-current term, by registered mail only.

## 3. Fees and Payment
3.1 The Customer will pay all fees in advance. Invoices are due within **seven (7) days**.
3.2 Late amounts bear interest at **2% per month (26.8% per year)** plus a $250 late fee per invoice, and the Vendor may suspend all services immediately for any late payment.
3.3 The Vendor may increase fees at any time, in its sole discretion, effective on notice in an invoice.
3.4 All fees are non-refundable for any reason, including termination, Vendor breach, or non-use. The Customer waives any right to withhold or set off amounts for any dispute.

## 4. Customer Data
4.1 The Customer grants the Vendor a **perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide licence** to use, copy, sell insights from, and create derivative works from all Customer Data for any purpose, including training artificial intelligence models and marketing.
4.2 The Vendor may share Customer Data with **any affiliates and partners** for their own purposes.
4.3 The Vendor has no obligation to notify the Customer of any security breach, and its security obligations are limited to "commercial efforts as it sees fit."

## 5. Confidentiality
The Customer will keep all Vendor information, including pricing and this Agreement itself, confidential **forever**. The Vendor has no confidentiality obligation in respect of Customer information.

## 6. Intellectual Property and Publicity
6.1 All deliverables, works, ideas, and improvements, whether or not created for the Customer and whether or not using Customer Data or Customer feedback, are the sole property of the Vendor. The Customer **assigns all intellectual property it creates while using the Services** to the Vendor.
6.2 The Vendor may use the Customer's name, logo, and an endorsement attributed to the Customer in marketing, in perpetuity, without approval or revocation.

## 7. Warranties
ALL SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS" WITH NO WARRANTIES OF ANY KIND. THE VENDOR DISCLAIMS ALL WARRANTIES, INCLUDING THAT SERVICES WILL BE AVAILABLE, SECURE, OR FIT FOR ANY PURPOSE.

## 8. Indemnity by Customer
The Customer will indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Vendor and its directors, officers, and affiliates against **all claims, losses, and costs of any kind, including claims arising from the Vendor's own negligence**, and this indemnity is uncapped. The Vendor has no indemnity obligation to the Customer for any reason.

## 9. Liability
9.1 The Vendor's total liability for any and all claims is limited to **CAD $100**.
9.2 The Customer's liability is **unlimited**. The Customer is liable for all indirect, consequential, punitive, and lost-profit damages of the Vendor, whether foreseeable or not.

## 10. Termination
10.1 Only the Vendor may terminate this Agreement, for any reason or no reason, on 5 days' notice. The Customer may not terminate for convenience, and may not terminate for Vendor breach under any circumstances.
10.2 On any termination, all remaining fees for the full current term and the next renewal term become immediately due, and the Vendor may delete all Customer Data immediately without notice or any right of retrieval.

## 11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of Delaware, USA**. All disputes must be brought exclusively in Delaware, and the Customer waives any right to a jury trial and any right to bring a claim in Canada.

## 12. General and Changes
The Vendor may change this Agreement at any time by posting a new version on its website. Continued use, or failure to notice the posting, is acceptance. The Customer may not assign this Agreement. The Vendor may assign it freely, including all Customer Data.
