# SAMPLE — One-Way Non-Disclosure Agreement (Discloser → Recipient)

> **SAMPLE FOR DEMONSTRATION AND TESTING IN VERICLAUSE ONLY.** Use this shape when only one side is sharing confidential information (e.g. a company sharing information with a prospective vendor or contractor). Not legal advice — have it reviewed before real use.

---

**NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (ONE-WAY)**

This Agreement is made on [DATE] between:

**Disclosing Party:** [FULL LEGAL NAME], of [ADDRESS] (the "Discloser")
**Receiving Party:** [FULL LEGAL NAME], of [ADDRESS] (the "Recipient")

## 1. Purpose
The Discloser will share confidential information with the Recipient so the Recipient can [DESCRIBE: e.g. "evaluate providing software development services"] (the "Purpose").

## 2. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means non-public information the Discloser shares for the Purpose that is marked confidential or should reasonably be understood as confidential, including [e.g. business, technical, product, pricing, and customer information].

## 3. What Does Not Count
Information is not Confidential Information if it: (a) becomes public without the Recipient's fault; (b) was already known to the Recipient free of a confidentiality duty; (c) comes from a third party free of such a duty; or (d) is independently developed without using the Discloser's information.

## 4. Recipient's Duties
The Recipient will: (a) use the information only for the Purpose; (b) share it only with representatives who need it for the Purpose and owe similar confidentiality duties; (c) protect it with at least reasonable care; and (d) not reverse-engineer any sample or prototype except as the Discloser permits in writing.

## 5. Legal Orders
If a court or regulator orders disclosure, the Recipient will, where lawful, tell the Discloser promptly, disclose only what is required, and help the Discloser limit the disclosure at the Discloser's expense.

## 6. How Long This Lasts
The Recipient's duties last for **three (3) years** from disclosure of the relevant information. [NOTE FOR TESTING: different NDA examples use different periods on purpose — VeriClause should read the actual number, not assume one.]

## 7. Return or Destruction
When the Purpose ends or the Discloser asks in writing, the Recipient will return or securely destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information and confirm this in writing on request, except copies that law or automatic backups require it to keep, which remain protected.

## 8. No Rights Granted
No intellectual property licence is granted. The Discloser makes no promise that the information is complete or accurate.

## 9. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable in Ontario, and disputes will be heard in Ontario courts.

## 10. General
This is the entire agreement on this subject. Changes require signed writing. Unenforceable parts do not affect the rest. Electronic signatures and counterparts are valid.
